On 13 November we held the 2026 Annual General Meeting of the IEEE Robotics and
Automation Society (RAS) Student Branch Chapter at the University of Moratuwa.
It closed out the 2025/26 term and handed things over to the 2026/27 executive
committee.

I spent the past year as Treasurer. Most of the time that meant budgets,
sponsor follow-ups, reimbursements, and keeping the accounts straight. It is
quiet work until an event week arrives and every small expense has to be sorted
out quickly.

## Highlights from Term 25/26

One of the events I was glad to see happen was MazeX 1.0, a Micromouse
competition that ran from workshops through to the competition itself. It was
something I had wanted to do for a long time and suggested early in the term.
I was out of Sri Lanka for most of the organising period and could only help
from a distance, so it was good to see the rest of the team carry it through.

We also ran a new event on Physical AI. The advisors appreciated the chapter
focusing on where robotics is headed and giving students early exposure to
something that will matter for them in the coming years.

## Handing over

At the AGM we welcomed Dr. Peshala Jayasekara as the new Chapter Advisor for
IEEE RAS UoM.
