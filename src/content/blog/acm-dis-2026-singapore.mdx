From 13 to 17 June I was in Singapore for ACM DIS 2026, the Designing
Interactive Systems conference hosted at the National University of Singapore.
It was my first academic conference, and I went with two pieces of work from my
research internship at the Exertion Games Lab at Monash University.

The theme was "Beyond Interaction", and a lot of the programme looked past
screens and standard interfaces toward ecological and more-than-human
relationships. Both of our projects fit right into that.

## Two papers at DIS 2026

During my internship at the Exertion Games Lab, we had two papers accepted to
the DIS 2026 Companion proceedings, both led by Hong Luo. I helped with the
technical implementation across hardware, electronics, and software:

- [SymbioSip: Human-Plant Symbiosis through a Bidirectional Hydration System](https://doi.org/10.1145/3802974.3808024)
  in the Interactivity track, an interactive system that links human and plant
  hydration. A wearable sensor detects when the wearer is dehydrated and
  triggers a pump to water an indoor plant, while a soil moisture sensor checks
  the plant and sends a light mist toward the wearer through a headband when
  the plant gets dry.
- [Listen to the Plant: Exploring the Design of Plant Care as an Auditory Interface to Promote Caregiving Behavior](https://doi.org/10.1145/3802974.3809437)
  in the Provocation / Work-in-Progress track, which looks at using sound as an
  interface for everyday plant care.

## People's Choice First Runner-Up

At the closing ceremony, SymbioSip won First Runner-Up for People's Choice
Interactivity. Watching people try the system and react to the plant-human
loop during the demo session was already the best part of the week, so seeing
the whole team recognised for it meant a lot.
